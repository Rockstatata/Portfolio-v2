## Design Context

### Users
Research collaborators and graduate labs, alongside recruiters and engineering teams. They need to understand Sarwad's research direction and find concrete engineering evidence quickly. The creator chose both audiences, with research depth and clear engineering proof.

### Brand Personality
Thoughtful, rigorous, inventive. The portfolio should explain how the work connects and invite exploration.

### Aesthetic Direction
Retain the established space setting, but tie the hero to the creator's story. An interactive research map connects software engineering, AI and GPU systems, and robotics. Space exploration is a stated long-term ambition, not a claim of completed space research.

The creator rejects thin wireframes, decorative orbit outlines, toy-like hardware miniatures, and cream/copper colors that conflict with the site. Use mature abstract surfaces in the established cyan, violet, and deep-blue palette, with quiet controls. Preserve continuity through smooth geometric transitions. Avoid faux instrument readouts and literal cartoon robots or satellites.

### Design Principles
- Give each visual element an explanatory or navigational purpose.
- Connect research ideas to portfolio evidence.
- Distinguish completed work from future ambitions.
- Keep the story readable without WebGL or animation.
- Support keyboard input, touch, and small screens.
- The creator explicitly requires always-on motion, including when the OS requests reduced motion. Use visible, smooth entrances, continuous hero movement, and consistent scroll inertia. Do not restore automatic motion suppression.
- Keep the animated galaxy visible behind the hero. Do not cover its center with opaque panel backgrounds or remove it during redesigns.
